# Medical Safety

MedEvidence Auditor was created by Jose Perez.

This is a MAMMAL evidence review and research safety layer.

This is not a medical device.

This is not clinical decision support.

This does not diagnose disease.

This does not treat or prescribe.

This does not recommend treatment.

This does not evaluate individual patient risk.

This does not replace licensed clinical judgment.

Outputs require review by qualified researchers, clinicians, or domain experts.

Synthetic/sample fixtures are not patient data.

Users are responsible for ethics, privacy, compliance, and regulatory review when using real datasets.
